Present Simple — Action Verbs & To Be
📚 Grammar Reference →

Action Verbs — Affirmative (Present Simple)

Structure
I / You / We / They + verb (base form)
He / She / It + verb + -s / -es / -ies
→ I work every day. / She works every day.
Examples
I play soccer on weekends.
They eat breakfast at 7 a.m.
He watches TV at night.
She studies English every day.
Spelling rules for He / She / It
Most verbs → add -s    (work → works)
-ch / -sh / -o / -x / -s → add -es    (watch → watches)
consonant + y → drop y, add -ies    (study → studies)
Time expressions
always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never
every day / week / year · on Mondays · in the morning

Action Verbs — Negative (Present Simple)

Structure
I / You / We / They + do not (don't) + verb (base)
He / She / It + does not (doesn't) + verb (base)
→ I don't eat meat. / He doesn't eat meat.
Examples
We don't play video games during school.
She doesn't drink coffee in the morning.
They don't travel every year.
It doesn't rain much in summer here.
⚠ Common mistake
✘ She doesn't works on Sundays.
✔ She doesn't work on Sundays.

Action Verbs — Questions (Present Simple)

Yes/No Questions
Do + I / you / we / they + verb (base) + ?
Does + he / she / it + verb (base) + ?
→ Do you like music? / Does she like music?
Short Answers
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Wh- Questions
Where do you live?
What does she do every morning?
How often do they visit their grandparents?
When does he go to the gym?

Verb To Be — Affirmative (Present Simple)

Forms
I → am     He / She / It → is     You / We / They → are
Contractions
I'm · he's · she's · it's · you're · we're · they're
Examples
I am a student.
She is very friendly.
They are from Colombia.
It is cold today.
Uses: descriptions, nationalities, professions, feelings, location
He is tired. · We are at school. · She is a doctor.

Verb To Be — Negative (Present Simple)

Structure
I am not (I'm not)
He / She / It is not (isn't)
You / We / They are not (aren't)
Examples
I am not happy today.
He isn't a teacher.
We aren't ready yet.
It isn't raining outside.

Verb To Be — Questions (Present Simple)

Yes/No Questions
Am I late?
Is he / she / it ready?
Are you / we / they students?
Short Answers
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Wh- Questions with To Be
Where are you from?
What is your favorite food?
Who is your best friend?
How old are you?
