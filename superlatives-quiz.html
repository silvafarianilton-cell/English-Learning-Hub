Rule: Use the + adjective + -est.
If it ends in consonant-vowel-consonant, double the last consonant.
big → the biggest  |  tall → the tallest  |  hot → the hottest
old → the oldest fast → the fastest cold → the coldest small → the smallest thin → the thinnest sad → the saddest
Rule: Use the most + adjective.
expensive → the most expensive  |  comfortable → the most comfortable
